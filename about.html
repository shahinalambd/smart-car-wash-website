About us

Car care, crafted in Dubai.

We started Smart Car Wash with a simple belief: every car deserves the attention of a showroom, every single visit.

Our story

Obsessed with detail from day one.

We began as a small team of car enthusiasts who wanted to offer the standard of a high-end detailing studio with the convenience of quick online booking. Today our Dubai studio brings together trained detailers, premium products and modern techniques.

We believe in transparent pricing, honest advice and results that speak for themselves.
